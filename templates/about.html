About Us

TheBus is the only public bus transportation service on the island of Oʻahu, Hawai'i.

We are privately managed by the nonprofit Oahu Transit Services Inc., which operates the system under a public-private partnership with the City and County of Honolulu's Department of Transportation Services. In 2024, TheBus had a ridership of 43,206,000, or about 134,800 per weekday, with a fleet comprised 518 buses and 207 paratransit vehicles.
